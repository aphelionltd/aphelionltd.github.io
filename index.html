01 / Services

What we do.

Aphelion is your trusted penetration testing partner. We provide independent security testing across your infrastructure, applications, cloud platforms and people, identifying vulnerabilities so they can be remediated before they are exploited.

Each engagement is scoped to your environment and delivered by experienced consultants, from a single web application to a full internal network and Active Directory assessment.

Infrastructure

External infrastructure

Everything you expose to the Internet, tested from the Internet: hosts, services and the DMZ.

Internal infrastructure

An attacker already inside. Privilege escalation, Active Directory and lateral movement.

Vulnerability assessment

Recurring scans with manual triage, compared period on period so you can see drift.

Wireless networks

Breaking into Wi-Fi, hunting rogue access points and checking segregation on site.

Network device review

Firewall, switch and router configs audited for weak crypto, credentials and segmentation.

Applications

Web applications

03 / Approach

How an engagement runs.

Every test follows the same structured process, agreed with you in writing before any testing begins.

  1. 01

    Scoping

    We confirm targets, objectives, testing windows and rules of engagement, then issue a scope of work and a project authorisation document for sign-off.

  2. 02

    Reconnaissance

    Passive and active information gathering to map the attack surface: domains, hosts, exposed services, applications and user accounts.

  3. 03

    Enumeration and analysis

    Service and version enumeration, configuration review and vulnerability scanning, with each result researched and verified manually.

  4. 04

    Exploitation

    Controlled exploitation to confirm real-world impact, including privilege escalation and lateral movement where in scope. Critical issues are reported to you on the day they are confirmed.

  5. 05

    Reporting

    A full technical report with an executive summary, delivered within five working days of testing completing.

  6. 06

    Debrief and retest

    A walkthrough of the results with your technical team, and optional retesting to confirm that remediation is effective.

04 / Reporting

A report you can act on.

Every finding is written up in the same structure, so your technical team knows exactly what is wrong, how serious it is and what to change.

  • CVSS v3.1 score, with impact and likelihood rated for your environment
  • MITRE ATT&CK techniques and mitigations mapped for your security team
  • Evidence captured during testing, redacted where it holds personal data
  • Specific remediation steps, not generic advice
